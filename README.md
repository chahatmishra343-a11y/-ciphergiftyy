### 💌 Why On Earth Did I Make This?
Once upon a time, people pressed flowers between pages. Today, we press memories into links.

People spend fortunes on physical flowers that wilt in days, yet the smallest message can remain alive for years. I engineered this platform so anyone can create a private little world that belongs to only two people—not because it is expensive, but because it is deeply personal.

### ✨ What Is In The Atelier? (The Gifts)
Every gift here begins as an empty canvas. You fill it with your own story, generate a custom link, and send it to your person.

* **[ 📝 THE_LETTER ]:** 95 editable templates for love, gratitude, or apologies.
* **[ 🌺 TEXT_BLOOM ]:** They tap the screen, flowers bloom across the glass, and your poetry unfolds.
* **[ 🎁 THE_GIFT_BOX ]:** A shaking, interactive 3D box that explodes with confetti to reveal a hidden photo and letter.
* **[ 🍬 THE_CANDY_JAR ]:** Fill a glass jar with virtual candies that burst out when opened to reveal a secret note.
* **[ 🎵 THE_MUSIC_CORNER ]:** A dedicated audio space to sing in your own voice, layered with falling petals.

### 💻 The Tech Stack (How it was cooked)

* **Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS:** Pure, elegant code to ensure every interactive element feels completely seamless.
* **HTML5 Canvas 2D API:** The math and physics engine powering the interactive `#petals-canvas` that literally dodges the user's mouse cursor.
* **Dynamic URL Engine:** A custom data-encoding architecture that instantly packages user input into secure, shareable payload links.
* **CSS Glassmorphism & Keyframes:** Delivering that ultra-premium, Pinterest-level Atelier aesthetic with perfect gradient fades and smooth 3D box-shakes.
