Welcome to the Atelier. 🕊️ I am **𝕮𝖎𝖕𝖍𝖊𝖗** (Chahat). I am not a florist. I do not arrange roses. I arrange feelings.

This repository is a master-hub for digital romance and connection. It is a collection of interactive, poetic web experiences designed to hold the words that are too heavy to carry and too beautiful to leave unsaid.

---

## 💌 𝖂𝖍𝖞 𝖔𝖓 𝕰𝖆𝖗𝖙𝖍 𝕯𝖎𝖉 𝕴 𝕸𝖆𝖐𝖊 𝕿𝖍𝖎𝖘?

Once upon a time, people pressed flowers between pages. Today, we press memories into links. 

People spend fortunes on physical flowers that wilt in days, yet the smallest message can remain alive for years. I wanted to build a garden that does not die. I engineered this platform so anyone can create a private little world that belongs to only two people—not because it is expensive, but because it is deeply personal.
